⚠️ Important

Account deletion cannot be undone. Your data is permanently deleted; we keep no backups, so it cannot be restored.

Cancel any subscription first: deleting the account does not cancel your Google Play or App Store subscription. Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Fingofy > Cancel subscription. On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Fingofy > Cancel Subscription.

📱 Delete in the app (immediate)

1
Open the Fingofy app

Sign in to your account

2
Go to the Profile tab

Tap "Profile" in the bottom menu

3
Tap "Delete My Account"

For security you will be asked to confirm your password or your Google/Apple sign-in again

4
Confirm

When you confirm with "Yes, Delete My Account", your account and data are deleted immediately

✉️ Can’t use the app? Request by email

If you cannot install the app or sign in, send an email from the email address registered to your Fingofy account to umaycorporation@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request.

  • We verify your identity by the request coming from the account’s registered email address. For requests from another address we may ask for additional verification.
  • We delete your account and data within 30 days at the latest and confirm by email when it is done.
  • You can also ask from the same address for specific data to be deleted without deleting your account.

🗑️ Data that is deleted

  • Your profile: first and last name, email, language and currency preferences, profile photo
  • Your accounts and balances, credit cards
  • Income/expense transactions, instalments, recurring and scheduled payments
  • Budgets, categories, goals and contributions
  • Debt/credit records and payments, vault (gold, currency, crypto) records, plans
  • Notifications, notification settings and device push tokens
  • Security and app logs linked to your account
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)

📦 Data we keep and for how long

  • Purchase verification record (product, order number, date): kept for 2 years with its link to your account removed, to stop the same purchase being used on several accounts at once. If you create a new Fingofy account with the same Google Play / App Store account, you can move your purchases to it with "Restore purchases".
  • Server system logs: deleted automatically within 30 days at most.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics; not linked to your account): deleted automatically within 90 days at most.
  • Purchase and billing records in Google Play and the App Store are Google’s and Apple’s responsibility and are not deleted by this process.

📋 Notes

  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
  • After deletion you can sign up again with the same email address; your old data does not come back.
